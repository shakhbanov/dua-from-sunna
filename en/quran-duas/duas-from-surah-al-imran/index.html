Duas from Surah Al 'Imran

For a steadfast heart

"Those endowed with understanding"

"Our Lord, do not let our hearts deviate after You have guided us, and grant us mercy from Yourself. Truly You are the Bestower."

When to recite: in doubt about one's faith; in a setting where faith is under pressure; daily, as a request for constancy.

[Quran 3:8]
